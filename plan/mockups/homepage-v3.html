Week 1 · Lesson 1

Start with the skill that changes every screen: hierarchy

Flip between before and after. The three rules below are what changed. You will practise each on a real screen in this lesson.

  1. 1

    Make one thing clearly the biggest

    The heading is the largest and heaviest thing on the screen, so the eye knows where to start.

  2. 2

    Let size and weight set the order

    Labels are semibold, helper text is lighter and grey. Three levels is enough. More makes the screen noisy.

  3. 3

    Give the screen one primary action

    One filled button. The second choice is outlined, the third is a plain link.

Do I need design experience or a design degree?

No. Phase 1 starts from first principles. You need a laptop, curiosity and about eight hours a week: six in live sessions and two on your own.

Do I have to pay for Figma?

No. Figma’s free plan covers everything in this course. We show you how to set up your files on day one.

What if I miss a live class?

Every class is recorded and posted the same day. You can also join the same session in the next batch at no extra cost.

Will I really have a portfolio at the end?

Yes. You publish three case studies, each reviewed by your mentor before it goes live, plus a simple portfolio site to hold them.

Do you guarantee a job?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. We give you the skills, the portfolio, mock interviews and introductions. The hiring decision is the company’s.
