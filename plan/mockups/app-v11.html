How every app page reads

  1. Page title + one line. Where am I and what is my situation, in one sentence ("Your access is active until 31 Aug 2027").
  2. The one dominant block. Dark (Continue, level) or ink-outlined (next class, subscription). It holds the page's only filled button.
  3. Supporting sections. H3 title on the left, a count or text link on the right, then cards or ruled rows.
  4. Quiet help. A WhatsApp text link or an outline button. Never a second filled button.
Why this sheet exists. The current app pages mix 13+ sizes, weights 500–800, 12–36px radii and teal chips. Here the hierarchy is fixed: page title (H2 role, 28–40px) → section title (H3, 21px) → card title (Body 600, 17px) → meta (Small 15px) → labels (14px). Each step is smaller and lighter or greyer, so the eye always knows what to read first.
