Why this shape

  • Old problem: no log out anywhere, and the learner area was a second row of tabs under the bar. Four links in the bar replace those tabs.
  • Log out is one click, no confirm dialog. It clears the session on this device only; "log out everywhere" lives in Password and devices.
  • Pending buyers (UPI waiting) and unpaid users see the same bar; Course material and Live classes show the unlock banner instead of lists.
  • Focus screens (checkout, player, viewers) replace the bar with logo + one exit, so there is nothing to wander off to mid-lesson.

Subscription · /account/subscription

  • Job: answer "do I have access, until when, and why?". No filled button (nothing to buy while active).

Password and devices · /account/security

  • Job: keep the account safe. One filled button: "Update password".
  • Update password: needs the current password; new password 8+ characters; on success all other sessions end and the learner gets an email.
  • Devices: the sessions of this account, newest first, with browser, city (from IP, approximate) and last seen. "Log out" on a row ends that session; "Log out of all other devices" ends the rest. The current device is marked and cannot be ended here (use Log out in the menu).
  • Email change: password first, then a 6-digit code to the new address; the old address gets a notice. Does not touch enrolments or the whitelist.
  • Learners do not get authenticator codes in v1 (staff accounts keep them, as built). Phone-code login is a later option.

Rules

  • Device limit: tenant_settings.security.device_limit already exists in the schema. If set (for example 2), a new login beyond it signs the oldest device out. Stops password sharing of a paid course. Value is an open question for the owner (plan/16 §10).
  • Forgot password lives on the login page and uses the emailed link.
