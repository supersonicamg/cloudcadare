What all four do differently from the current hero

  • Small corners (3–6px), not 18–20px. Big round corners plus pastel panels is what made it feel like a kids' app.
  • Weight 600 with tight tracking for the headline, not 800. The size does the work. Everything else stays at 400–500.
  • No highlight bars, line drawings or icon chips in the hero. Information comes from words, numbers and one real photo or table.
  • Everything sits on one 12-column grid, so the edges line up. Thin rules separate things instead of boxes and shadows.
  • One accent colour per direction, one filled button, and WhatsApp as a plain text link. The header button is always an outline.
  • Every number, name, date and photo is a placeholder, listed at the top of the file.
