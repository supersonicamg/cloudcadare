Sign up · /signup

  • Fields: full name, email, WhatsApp number (+91, 10 digits), password (8+ characters). WhatsApp number is required: payment help and class reminders go there, and staff match screenshots by it.

Why the code comes before the whitelist

  • The whitelist gives access by email alone. If we granted access on a typed email, anyone who knows a learner's address could sign up as them and take the access.
  • So every new account confirms its email first. Same screen for everyone, so the form never reveals whether an email is on the list.
  • This changes today's setting "Confirm email: off" (PROGRESS.md). It must be turned on for the cloud project. See plan/16 §4.

Checkout · /checkout/[orderId]

  • Job: pay with confidence. One filled button: "I have paid" (UPI). Focus layout: logo only, no navigation.
  • The order exists before this page (orders.status = 'awaiting_payment', order no CC-2026-000123 from tenant_counters). Price, GST and coupon are fixed on the order; the page only reads them.
  • UPI QR: live. Razorpay: "Coming soon", not selectable, no click target, aria-disabled. It switches on later when the owner adds Razorpay keys (admin → Payment settings); no code change.
  • The QR encodes a UPI link with the exact amount and the order number as the payment note: upi://pay?pa=cloudcadre@okaxis&pn=CloudCadre&am=49999.00&tn=CC-2026-000123&cu=INR. If the owner prefers one static QR, the page shows the amount and the note to type.
  • On a phone the page also shows "Open UPI app" (the same link), because a learner cannot scan their own screen.

What "I have paid" does

  • Creates payments row: gateway = 'upi_manual', status = 'created', amount = order total. Order stays awaiting_payment. A payment_proofs row is opened with state pending_review.
  • Notifies staff (admin badge on "UPI payments", optional WhatsApp or email to the owner).
  • Goes to the "Waiting for verification" page. Pressing it twice must not create two payments (idempotency key = order id + "upi").
  • The learner can come back and re-open the QR until the order is paid or cancelled (orders unpaid for 7 days are cancelled).

Payment could not be matched · same URL, state needs_action

  • Staff reject with one reason from a fixed list, plus an optional note. The learner sees the matching text: amount_mismatch (shows what we saw and what is due), not_received, screenshot_unclear, wrong_account, duplicate.
  • Order stays awaiting_payment; the proof is rejected (kept for audit). Nothing is refunded by the app: money that arrived by mistake is returned by hand, and staff record it as a refund on the order.
  • Underpaid: the one filled button opens a new QR for only the missing amount (second payment on the same order). The order is paid when the sum of captured payments reaches the total.
  • Other reasons: the filled button is "Send the screenshot again".
  • Secondary action is always WhatsApp to a person.

Rules

  • No red and no scolding copy: say what we saw and what to do next.
  • A learner can have only one open proof per order. A new upload supersedes the rejected one.
