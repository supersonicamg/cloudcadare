Course material · /learn/ceap/material

  • Job: find any lesson, file or recording fast. No filled button: every row is a link.
  • Four kinds of material, one list, grouped by month: Videos (lesson.type = 'video'), Live recordings ('live' with a recording), PDFs ('pdf', 'document'), Images ('image'). Counts in the chips are for the whole program.
  • Filter chips are single-select; search matches lesson titles and the learner's own note text. "All" is the default.
  • Row states: tick = completed, New (yellow) = published in the last 7 days and not opened, progress % = started video, lock + date = unlock_rule not met yet, pencil + n = my notes on it.
  • The month the learner is in opens by default; finished months are collapsed; locked months show titles but not links.

Rules

  • Access = an active enrolment (my_access RPC, already built). Without one the list shows the first lessons marked is_preview and a banner "Unlock CEAP".
  • Files and recordings are served by signed URLs that expire in minutes (/api/lesson-file/[lessonId]). A PDF with allow_download = false opens in the viewer with no download button.
  • Unpublished items are invisible. Staff upload and order them in the lesson editor (no new admin screen).

Recording player with notes · /learn/[course]/[lessonId]

  • Job: learn without distraction. Focus layout (logo + one exit). One filled button: "Mark complete"; next lesson is an outline button.
  • Take a note: the note box stamps the current video time (position_seconds). The timestamp chip is a button that seeks the video. Press N to focus the box and pause; Ctrl+Enter saves.
  • Mark this moment = a bookmark (kind = 'bookmark', optional text). Notes and bookmarks both show as yellow ticks on the scrubber, so the learner sees where they marked things.
  • Notes are private to the learner (RLS: member_id = me). Edit and delete are inline; delete asks nothing but offers "Undo" for 6 seconds.
  • Notes autosave as drafts in the browser while typing and are saved to the server on "Save note" (offline-safe).

Progress and resume

  • Heartbeat every 15 s writes position through the existing progress API; reopening resumes at that second (minus 3 s).
  • Complete automatically at 90% watched, or by "Mark complete". Completing moves the learner to the next lesson only if they press Next.
  • Tabs in the side panel: Notes (this lesson), Chapters (from the lesson's chapter list), Class list (lessons around this one).
  • Phone: the panel sits under the video; the note box stays pinned above the keyboard.
  • Free preview lessons (is_preview) play for signed-out visitors but have no Notes tab: it shows "Log in to take notes".

My notes · /learn/notes

  • Job: find something I wrote or marked, then jump back to that exact minute or page. No filled button.
  • Everything from lesson_notes for this learner, grouped by lesson (newest lesson first), filtered by kind (Notes, Bookmarks, Highlights) and searchable by text and lesson title.
  • Each item links to the lesson with ?t=1634 (seconds) or ?page=3; the player opens there.
  • "Download as PDF" builds one document grouped by month and lesson with timestamps, so learners can revise offline. Generated on request, not stored.

Rules

  • Notes are private. Trainers and admins cannot read them.
  • Deleting a lesson (staff) keeps the learner's notes hidden, not deleted, for 30 days.
  • If access ends (expiry or refund) notes stay readable and exportable for 30 more days, but lessons are locked.
