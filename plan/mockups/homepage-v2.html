What a CloudCadre batch looks like

You always know what happens this week, who is teaching it and what you will have built by Sunday.

1

Live classes

Three to six hours a week with your trainer, on a timetable you see before you pay. Missed one? It is recorded.

2

Hands-on labs

You build on a real cloud account from week one. Your trainer reviews each lab; nothing is auto-ticked.

3

A capstone project

Design and defend a production-style system in front of your trainer. It goes on your CV and portfolio.

4

Interview preparation

Mock interviews with working engineers, CV reviews, and introductions to companies that hire our learners.

The capstone

You finish by building something real

Every AWS learner designs, deploys and defends the same kind of system a company would run in production.

  • A highly available web app behind a CDN and load balancer
  • Auto Scaling servers and a managed database, all in your own network
  • Costs tagged and reviewed, so you can explain the bill

Do I need an IT background?

No. Foundation courses start from Linux and networking basics. If you come from a non-IT job, we include a free two-week foundation module before your batch starts.

What if I miss a live class?

Every class is recorded and posted the same day. You can also attend the same session in the next batch at no extra cost.

Are certification exam fees included?

No. Exam fees are paid to the exam provider. We cover the preparation, practice tests and a mock exam in the final fortnight.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Every plan can be split into monthly instalments, with no hidden interest on 6-month plans.

Do you guarantee a job?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. We give you the skills, a portfolio project, mock interviews and introductions; the hiring decision is the company's.
