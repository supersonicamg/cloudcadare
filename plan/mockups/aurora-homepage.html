Do I need an IT background?

No. Foundation courses start from Linux and networking basics. If you come from a non-IT job, begin with a foundation course and move on from there.

Can I try a course before I buy it?

Yes. Download the free sample lesson to see how we teach, or ask a counsellor on WhatsApp to walk you through the syllabus.

How do I pay, and can I use instalments?

Pay online by UPI, card or net banking. Instalments are available on every course.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes, a certificate on completion that employers can verify online. Exam fees for AWS and Azure are paid to the exam provider; we prepare you with practice tests.

Do you guarantee a job?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. We give you the skills, a portfolio project, mock interviews and introductions; the hiring decision is the company's.
