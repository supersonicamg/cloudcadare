Do I need an IT background?

No. Foundation courses start from Linux and networking basics. If you come from a non-IT job, begin with a foundation course; a counsellor can tell you which one.

Are classes live or recorded?

Live, two or three evenings a week, with the recording up the same night. Labs you do in your own time on a real cloud account.

How do I pay, and can I use instalments?

Pay online by UPI, card or net banking. Every course can be paid in monthly instalments.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes, a certificate on completion that employers can verify online. AWS and Azure exam fees are paid to the exam provider; we prepare you with practice tests.

Do you guarantee a job?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. We give you the skills, a portfolio project, mock interviews and introductions; the hiring decision is the company’s.
