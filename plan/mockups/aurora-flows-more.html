What “the cloud” really is

Regions, zones and edge locations

A plain-language start: what you rent, who runs it, and what you are responsible for.

0:00
8:12
The video for this lesson is not ready yet.

AWS is split into Regions, and each Region is made of Availability Zones: separate data centres a few kilometres apart with their own power and network.

Why it matters

Spread a workload over two zones and one failure no longer takes you down. Edge locations sit closer to your users and cache content so pages load faster.
