    Questions before you join

    Do I need an IT background?

    No. Month 1 starts from cloud basics. If you are completely new to IT, a counsellor will suggest a short warm-up first.

    How many hours a week?

    About 8 to 10: two or three live evening classes, plus labs in your own time.

    What if I miss a class?

    Every class is recorded and up the same night, and your mentor helps you catch up on the lab.

    Do I get a certificate?

    Yes, a CEAP certificate employers can verify online. AWS, Azure and Google Cloud exam fees are paid to the exam provider.

    Do you guarantee a job?

    No, and be wary of anyone who does. We give you the skills, a capstone, mock interviews, CV and LinkedIn help, and introductions.
